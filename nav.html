About Pio De Roda

Pio De Roda Cafe, Restaurant And Catering Services began as a small neighborhood coffee corner and grew into a gathering place for everyday meals and once-in-a-lifetime celebrations alike. Our kitchen and events team work side by side, so the same care that goes into a cup of coffee also goes into every wedding, reunion, and birthday we help host.

Whether you are planning an intimate get-together or a full-scale celebration, our goal is the same: good food, dependable service, and a space that feels like it was set up just for you.

  • Catering & EventsFull-service packages for gatherings of any size
  • Venue & RentalsA space and equipment ready for your occasion
  • Dine-In MenuEveryday meals for guests joining before or after the event
